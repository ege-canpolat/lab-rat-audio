Elsewhere teleports your track to six places people actually hear music: through your neighbour's wall, the club toilets, the smoking area, a parked car, a phone on the kitchen table and a festival campsite.

This installs:

  • VST3 for Ableton Live, FL Studio, Bitwig, Reaper, Cubase and friends
  • Audio Unit for Logic Pro and GarageBand

Close your music app before you continue. Works on macOS 11 or newer, on Apple silicon and Intel Macs.
